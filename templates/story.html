Scene 2 — Age & the Risk Factor
Heart Disease by Age Group (%)

Age Drives Risk — Significantly

As patients age, the prevalence of chronic diseases rises sharply. Patients in the 61+ age group show significantly higher rates of Heart Disease, Diabetes, and Stroke compared to younger cohorts.

Dr. Michael Carter uses this insight to prioritize screening for older patients and establish age-based early intervention protocols.

💡 Insight: Age is the strongest single demographic predictor of chronic disease in this dataset.
Scene 3 — Lifestyle Habits & Disease

Smoking, Alcohol & Exercise

Lifestyle behaviors have a measurable impact on disease outcomes. Smokers and alcohol consumers show elevated rates of all three chronic conditions. Conversely, patients with higher exercise levels carry significantly lower average risk scores.

Emily Rodriguez leverages these correlations to design targeted wellness programs for high-risk behavioral segments.

⚠️ Key Finding: Sedentary patients carry ~25% higher risk scores than active patients.

Scene 4 — Clinical Indicators
Clinical Averages — Heart Disease vs No Disease

Blood Pressure, Cholesterol & Glucose

Patients with Heart Disease consistently show elevated readings across all four clinical markers: Blood Pressure, Cholesterol, Glucose, and BMI. The radar chart reveals the clinical fingerprint of high-risk patients.

Early detection of elevated cholesterol and glucose levels can prompt preventive interventions, reducing the risk of disease progression.

📌 Clinical Tip: Combined elevation of BP + Cholesterol + Glucose is a strong multi-marker risk signal.
Scene 5 — Symptom Patterns

Fatigue, Chest Pain & Dizziness

Symptom analysis reveals which clinical complaints are most prevalent in the population. Chest Pain is the most commonly reported symptom, followed by Fatigue and Dizziness.

These symptom patterns help Dr. Priya Nair design population-wide screening programs and allocate healthcare resources to the right patient segments.

🔬 Research Note: Patients reporting Chest Pain + Dizziness together show 3× higher stroke risk.
